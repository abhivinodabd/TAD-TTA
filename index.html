Timestep-Aware Diffusion Denoiser Scheduling for Text-to-Audio Generation

Abhinav Vinod and Srikanth Raj Chetupalli
Indian Institute of Technology Bombay
💻 Code

Abstract

Diffusion models achieve strong text-to-audio generation quality but require expensive denoiser evaluations. In this study, we investigate efficient text-to-audio diffusion using timestep-aware multi-network inference. We derive a collection of denoisers with varying computational and memory complexities by pruning AudioLDM2 and then study the required denoiser capacity at different steps along the diffusion trajectory. Our results show that early, highly noisy stages tolerate substantial model compression, whereas later stages are more capacity-sensitive. Accordingly, scheduling denoisers of different capacities across the reverse process provides a better quality-efficiency trade-off than using a single compact denoiser throughout. Objective evaluations on AudioCaps and Clotho, together with subjective evaluation on AudioCaps, show that the timestep-aware denoiser scheduling reduces inference cost while retaining strong generation quality and text-audio correspondence.
